The Orenda Foundation

The part of Orenda built to give back.

It's the giving arm of everything we're building, sister-led and rooted in the same land as the farm. What the work earns and the land grows, we turn back toward food, farmers, financial footing, and the well-being of the people around us.

Our mission
Our Mission

Why the Foundation exists

The Orenda Foundation is how we give back. As the farm and our work grow, we send a share of what they produce into the causes that keep a community whole — food that reaches the table, farmers healing their land, people learning to hold their own money, and the wellness of the living things in our care.

We built it on a plain promise: as we grow, we give; as we produce, we restore.
